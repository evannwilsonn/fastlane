    How it works

    Every car starts with the smallest possible brain: sensors wired straight to the controls. Each generation the best brains breed, and mutations can add neurons and connections, so the network grows as the levels get harder. Every three levels the road changes: two lanes, curves, construction zones, weather, new places.
